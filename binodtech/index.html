BinodTech

Also known as Binod Suman — architect, YouTuber, and tech educator.

BinodTech is the name people use for Binod Suman’s tech work: system design, DSA, AI, and interview study material published at binodsuman.com and on YouTube @binodsuman.

Not BindTech (concrete)

Google sometimes treats “BinodTech” as a typo for BindTech. BindTech / bindtech.co.in is a concrete research company in Chennai. BinodTech is software education — YouTube videos and free interview notes — by Binod Suman.
