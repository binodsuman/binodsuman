Top Behavioral Interview Questions

Questions asked at Amazon, Google, Microsoft + what a strong answer signals.

Interview tip Amazon loves Leadership Principles — align each answer to one LP explicitly.

QuestionThey want to see

Red flags to avoid

  • Blaming others in failure stories
  • Vague "we did" without your role
  • No metrics in results
  • Speaking negatively about past employers
