STAR Method — Master Behavioral Answers

Structure every story in 2 minutes: Situation, Task, Action, Result — with examples for tech roles.

Interview tip Spend 10% on Situation, 10% Task, 60% Action (what YOU did), 20% Result (numbers if possible).

STAR framework

S — Situation: 1–2 sentences context
T — Task: your responsibility
A — Action: steps YOU took (bulk of answer)
R — Result: outcome + learning

Example (technical leadership)

S: Kafka consumer lag spiked during peak traffic.
T: I owned the streaming pipeline as senior engineer.
A: Profiled consumers, found slow deserialization, added parallel processing, tuned partition count, added lag alerts.
R: Lag dropped 95%, zero missed SLA for 6 months; runbook shared with team.
